Historical Context: The PT-109 wireless message was sent after Lt. John F. Kennedy's patrol boat was rammed by the Japanese destroyer Amagiri in August 1943.
Key Used: "ROYAL NEW ZEALAND NAVY" constructs the 5x5 Playfair grid (with I/J combined).
Decryption Process: Each digraph is decrypted by shifting left on the same row, shifting up on the same column, or taking opposite corners of the rectangle.
Decoded Text: "PT BOAT ONE ZERO NINE LOST IN ACTION IN BLACKETT STRAIT TWO MILES SW MERESU COVE X CREW OF TWELVE X REQUEST ANY INFORMATION"
